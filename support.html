从这里开始。

关于同步、导出、购买、权限与支持系统的常见问题，都可以在这里找到答案。

Q1打开了 iCloud 同步，另一台设备上没有出现笔记

按顺序排查，绝大多数是前三项：

  • 开关是按设备分别保存的。新设备上也要单独打开一次，不会自动跟着过来。
  • 切换开关后需要重新打开 App 才生效。设置页会提示「将在重新打开 App 后生效」。
  • 系统层要先开着 iCloud。到「系统设置 → 你的姓名 → iCloud」确认已登录，且本 App 的开关是打开的。
  • 给它一点时间。导入是渐进的，首次同步可能需要几秒到几分钟，期间请保持 App 在前台。
  • 还不行，就彻底退出 App 再打开。从后台完全划掉 App（不只是回到主屏幕），再重新打开，会再触发一次同步检查。

如果设置页出现红色提示，展开「查看原因与操作」，里面有对应原因与「复制诊断信息」。

Q2关掉同步，云端的数据会消失吗

不会。关闭开关只是停止继续同步，已经写入 iCloud 的数据仍然留在你的账户里，重开开关就会回来。如果只想删除某篇笔记，在 App 内删除即可，删除状态会同步到其他设备；如果想清除本 App 在 iCloud 中的全部历史记录，需到「系统设置 → 你的姓名 → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间」中删除本 App 的数据。

Q3换手机 / 换电脑，笔记怎么迁过去

两种方式，任选其一：

  • 走 iCloud：两台设备登录同一个 Apple 账户，都打开同步开关，等首次同步完成。
  • 走导出：旧设备上导出 Markdown 包（含图片与附件），存到「文件」或网盘，再在新设备上导入。

为了安全起见，建议先备份再换机。换机前请在旧设备上导出一次 Markdown 包并妥善保存——这是唯一不依赖网络、也不依赖同步是否顺利的保险。确认新设备已导入成功，再考虑清理旧设备。

Q4换机后功能没解锁 / 怎么恢复购买

本 App 为一次性买断，购买记录绑定你的 Apple 账户。在新设备上确认登录同一个 Apple 账户，到买断页点击「恢复购买」即可，不会重复扣费。若仍无法恢复，请确认 App Store 处于登录状态后再试。

Q5拍照 / 录音用不了

到「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 相机 / 麦克风」确认本 App 已开启。如果之前点过「不允许」，在这里重新打开即可，App 不会因此丢失任何已有笔记。

Q6导出的文件在哪里

导出时可选择保存到本地或 iCloud Drive。导出内容为 Markdown 文档与附件文件夹，任何支持 Markdown 的编辑器都能打开，不依赖本 App。

Q7支持哪些系统

iPhone 与 iPad 需 iOS / iPadOS 17.6 或更高；Mac 需 macOS 15.4 或更高。iPhone 版为竖屏布局，iPad 与 Mac 支持更宽的排版。

Q1I turned on iCloud sync, but my notes aren't showing up on another device

Work through these in order — nearly every case is one of the first three:

  • The switch is saved per device. Turn it on separately on the new device; it won't carry over by itself.
  • Changing the switch needs an app restart. Settings shows "Takes effect after reopening the app."
  • iCloud must be on at the system level. Go to Settings → your name → iCloud and confirm you're signed in and this app's switch is on.
  • Give it a moment. Importing is gradual — the first sync can take anywhere from a few seconds to a few minutes. Keep the app in the foreground.
  • Still nothing? Force-quit and reopen the app. Swipe it fully away from the app switcher (not just back to the Home Screen), then open it again to trigger another sync check.

If Settings shows a red warning, expand "View reason and actions" — it lists the cause along with a "Copy diagnostic info" option.

Q2If I turn off sync, will my cloud data be deleted?

No. Turning the switch off only stops further syncing; data already written to iCloud stays in your account and comes back when you turn sync on again. To delete a single note, just delete it in the app — the deletion syncs to your other devices. To erase all of this app's cloud history, remove the app's data under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Q3How do I move my notes to a new phone or Mac?

Either method works:

  • Via iCloud: sign in to the same Apple account on both devices, turn on sync on each, and wait for the first sync to finish.
  • Via export: export a Markdown package (including photos and attachments) on the old device, save it to Files or cloud storage, then import it on the new device.

To be safe, back up before switching devices. Before switching devices, export a Markdown package on the old one and keep it somewhere safe — it's the only fallback that depends on neither the network nor whether sync behaved. Once the new device has imported successfully, then consider wiping the old one.

Q4Features aren't unlocked after switching devices / how do I restore my purchase?

Thinkery is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple account. Sign in to the same Apple account on the new device and tap "Restore Purchase" on the unlock page — you won't be charged again. If it still doesn't restore, make sure the App Store is signed in and try once more.

Q5Camera or recording doesn't work

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera / Microphone and make sure Thinkery is enabled. If you previously chose "Don't Allow", turn it back on there — no existing notes are lost either way.

Q6Where do exported files go?

You can save exported files locally or to iCloud Drive. The export contains Markdown documents and an attachments folder — any Markdown editor can open them, with no need for this app.

Q7Which operating systems are supported?

iPhone and iPad require iOS / iPadOS 17.6 or later; Mac requires macOS 15.4 or later. iPhone uses a portrait layout, while iPad and Mac offer wider layouts.
